A Deep Dive

Something I’ve Built

Although I’ve been playing around with numerous AI models, agent builders, and app designers, there is one specific agent that I built that I want to talk about. This is an agent that I built this summer in my consulting internship at Baringa, where I sat in the enterprise value creation team as a strategy analyst.

One offering our team provided to clients was benchmarking their capability in a certain function area against their competitors and the industry standard. This process was being done manually, taking a lot of time and usually not offering very unique insight to take to the client. Seeing this process as something that could be significantly improved, I took initiative and proactively built a benchmarking agent.

Building in complex reasoning processes to create a hallucination free, high quality, and visualised output, the agent finally reached a stage where I was happy to present it to my colleagues. After showing leading AI figures in the firm how I built it and what it does, iterating it on their feedback, I then ran directors through how I used it. They gave me really positive feedback, and ended up actually using it in a live client request.

This was the first intern-led AI initiative to be used on a live client request, and I learnt the importance of addressing potential points of improvement, taking initiative, and building!

What I’m Proud Of

My work ethic. This is something that I have held since I first realised the level of ambition that I had when I was 17. Since then, I have focused on achieving the highest academic grades I could. The hard work first paid off when I turned my grades around, going from a B+ student to achieving straight A*’s at A-Levels, including achieving the highest grade in the country in Human Geography.

This gave me the confidence going into university to carry on aiming high. With a solidified mentality of dedication to hard work, I achieved a first class grade in every year of university. I also achieved a High Distinction (averaging 81%) during my study abroad year at Monash University in Melbourne, which taught me valuable lessons beyond academia (but more on that later).

In my final year, I committed to one final push to finish with a strong First Class degree, which I did. I graduated as one of the top of my cohort, and received the faculty academic excellence award for my dissertation (graded 92%). My paper researched how to create a productive group environment that enables individuals to be more creative and confident in their output.

This background of academic knowledge, and work ethic that I developed, is something that I am now looking to take forward into my career.
